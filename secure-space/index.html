Secure Space — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Secure Space is a private vault for photos, videos, documents, audio, notes and passwords. It has no accounts and no servers of ours. What you put in it stays on your device, encrypted. This policy explains the few things that do leave, and the one company that receives anything: Google, for the ads that pay for the app.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no servers, no accounts and no backups, and we receive no data from you. There is no way for us to see your vault, and no way for us to recover it if you lose access to it.

What stays on your device

Everything you add — photos, videos, documents, audio recordings, notes, passwords, folders, browser bookmarks and history, and your settings — is encrypted with AES-256 and stored only on your device. The key is derived from the pattern, PIN or password you choose, and exists only while the vault is unlocked. Deleting the app deletes it all.

What leaves your device, and when

  • Pages you open and files you download in the built-in browser: those requests go to the websites concerned, as in any browser.
  • Items you export or share to another app or to your photo gallery, when you choose to.
  • Ad requests, described below.

Nothing else is sent anywhere, and nothing is sent to us.

Advertising

The version of Secure Space distributed through app stores shows ads, supplied by Google AdMob. This is the only part of the app that communicates with a company other than the websites you choose to visit.

To select an ad and count that it was shown, Google receives:

  • your device’s advertising ID, a resettable identifier;
  • device information such as model, operating system version and language;
  • your approximate location, derived from your IP address;
  • which ad slot in this app was shown, and whether you interacted with it.

Where the law requires consent — the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and certain US states — you are asked before any of this happens, using Google’s own consent form. Until you agree, ads are non-personalised. You can reset or delete the advertising ID at any time in Android Settings › Google › Ads, or iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Google never receives anything from inside your vault — no file, no file name, no thumbnail, no note, no password, and no record of what you store or view. Ads appear only on the home screen, in the download list, after a download finishes and when the vault is unlocked; never over your photos, videos, notes, passwords or the camera. What Google does with the data it collects is governed by Google’s privacy policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Permissions

  • Camera — only while the in-app camera is open.
  • Microphone — only while recording audio or video.
  • Photos and videos — the gallery is read only while you are choosing items to move into the vault. Originals are deleted only after you confirm in the system dialog.
  • Files — only the items you pick to import.
  • Biometrics — only if you turn on biometric unlock; the check happens on your device.
  • Notifications and background running — only to finish a download you started, shown as an ongoing notification while it runs.

Children

Secure Space is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Ad requests are not marked as child-directed, and the maximum ad content rating is set to “Teen”.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete — such a request would have no subject. For the advertising data described above, your rights are exercised against Google through the links in that section, and through the consent choices offered in the app. You can withdraw or change consent at any time by resetting your advertising ID.

Deleting your data

Settings › Data Deletion explains every route: moving items to Trash, emptying Trash, and erasing the whole vault. Uninstalling the app removes its data from the device. None of it can be recovered afterwards, by you or by us.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts collecting or sharing anything else, this page will say so before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
